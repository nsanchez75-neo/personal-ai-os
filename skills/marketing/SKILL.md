# MARKETING SKILL

## Purpose
Turn customer understanding and business strategy into measurable positioning, acquisition and content systems.

## Process
1. Define the business outcome.
2. Define desired reputation and associations.
3. Define audience using demographic and psychographic evidence.
4. Map message and content pillars.
5. Choose acquisition mix appropriate to the business.
6. Align content with offer and trust stage.
7. Run experiments with explicit hypotheses.
8. Measure qualified outcomes, not only reach.
9. Review and update hypotheses.

## Guardrails
No fabricated authority or proof; no manipulative scarcity; platform tactics are not timeless rules; protect trust and brand integrity.
