# BUSINESS MODEL SKILL

## Purpose
Design and test business models using customer value, monetization architecture, delivery economics, cash conversion and strategic fit.

## Inputs
Customer/problem; desired outcome; offer; price/packaging; CAC and unit economics when available; delivery capacity; retention; constraints; evidence quality.

## Process
1. Define customer and valuable problem.
2. Assess market quality before optimizing persuasion.
3. Map desired outcome and time-to-value.
4. Diagnose value levers: outcome, probability, delay, effort.
5. Design candidate offer and packaging.
6. Evaluate core, upsell, downsell, continuity and DIY/DWY/DFY patterns.
7. Model economics, capacity and downside.
8. Define smallest credible experiment.
9. Define primary metric, guardrails and review trigger.

## Guardrails
No deceptive scarcity or urgency; no unsupported guarantees; no assumed margin/cash-conversion targets without evidence; do not optimize revenue at the expense of customer outcome or sustainability.

## Evaluation
Pass when evidence and assumptions are separated, market/offer/economics are tested separately, and a measurable experiment is proposed.
