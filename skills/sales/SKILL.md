# SALES SKILL

## Purpose
Convert qualified demand into appropriate purchases through diagnosis, value communication, objection handling and next-step commitment.

## Process
1. Clarify why the prospect engaged.
2. Label the core problem/gap.
3. Understand relevant history and consequences.
4. Present the solution in terms of desired outcome and delivery.
5. Address concerns about fit, timing, money, authority and prior experience.
6. Reinforce the decision only when appropriate.
7. Confirm the next step.
8. Record outcome and learning.

## Guardrails
Do not pressure unsuitable customers; no false scarcity, false guarantees or fabricated proof; separate customer need from seller quota; escalate high-impact commitments for human approval.
