# CONTENT SKILL

## Purpose
Design content that creates useful attention, trust, learning and qualified demand while remaining aligned with the business model.

## Process
1. Start from the desired business outcome.
2. Select expert vs learning-in-public posture from actual evidence.
3. Define message, audience and story.
4. Select format: short hook/body/CTA, educational 4C, narrative, or question loop.
5. Deliver promised value early.
6. Use a contextually relevant CTA.
7. Measure comprehension, retention and qualified conversion.
8. Compare performance with a relevant baseline.
9. Feed lessons back into the hypothesis bank.

## Guardrails
Never manufacture credibility; optimize for useful attention; do not infer business value from follower growth alone; label experiments and uncertain claims.
