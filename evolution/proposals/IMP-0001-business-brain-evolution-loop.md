# IMP-0001 — Business Brain Evolution Loop

Status: ACCEPTED as architecture.

Business Brain discoveries are the first input to a shared Evolution Loop. The loop separates learning from mutation, requires sandbox evaluation and regression checks, and preserves human approval for high-impact or irreversible promotion.

The implementation also adds a Business Brain context-integrity gate so malformed test context cannot be misclassified as model behavior.

This is architectural/runtime guardrail work; it does not constitute LLM behavioral qualification.
