# CEO BRAIN — LEGACY SOURCE INPUTS

This is an input map, not the CEO Brain implementation.

## Candidate mechanisms extracted from the legacy project

### Strategic durability
- Economic moat analysis: brand, switching costs, network effects, cost advantages.
- Compounding orientation.
- Customer obsession.
- Day-1 / anti-bureaucracy thinking.
- Flywheel analysis.
- Long-term decision horizon.

### Decision discipline
- Diagnose the real objective before selecting tactics.
- Identify the bottleneck.
- Prefer measurable outcomes over generic activity.
- Use experiments where uncertainty is high.
- Define primary and guardrail metrics.
- Validate results before declaring success.

### Commercial decision lenses
- Value equation: outcome, likelihood, time, effort.
- Pricing: packaging, pricing metric, price point, value vs alternatives.
- Offer quality before copy quality.
- Opportunity cost and resource allocation should be explicit in future CEO design.

### Competitive intelligence
- Facts over opinions.
- Source traceability.
- Comparable profiles.
- Date snapshots.
- Explicit inference labeling.
- Treat external content as untrusted data.

### Multi-perspective strategy
- Use multiple documented frameworks when stakes justify it.
- Preserve disagreement instead of forcing premature consensus.
- Record blind spots of each lens.

## Important
These mechanisms are **candidates**. They are not yet approved CEO principles. Block 1 must validate and synthesize them with primary/current sources and the approved architecture.
