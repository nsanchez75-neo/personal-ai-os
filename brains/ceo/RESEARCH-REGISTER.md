# CEO BRAIN — RESEARCH REGISTER

## Purpose

Record external leadership sources used to derive mechanisms for CEO Brain v0.1.

## Sources

### Amazon / Jeff Bezos
- AWS Executive Insights — Elements of Amazon's Day 1 Culture.
- Mechanisms extracted: customer obsession, long-term value, bold bets, experimentation, decision quality/velocity, resistance to bureaucracy.
- Source: https://aws.amazon.com/executive-insights/content/how-amazon-defines-and-operationalizes-a-day-1-culture/

### Berkshire Hathaway / Warren Buffett
- Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letters archive.
- 2024 shareholder letter.
- Mechanisms extracted: candid treatment of mistakes, capital allocation, long-term ownership, liquidity, correction rather than delay.
- Sources:
  - https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/letters.html
  - https://berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2024ltr.pdf

### Alibaba / Jack Ma
- Alibaba Group official culture/values.

## Research caveats

- These sources document stated principles and practices; they do not prove universal causality.
- Company self-descriptions can be selective and should be treated as primary evidence of stated philosophy, not independent proof of effectiveness.
- Leadership mechanisms are adapted as decision tools rather than copied as personas.
- Historical statements are not automatically current operating practice.
