## High-value mechanisms

### 1. Brand Journey
Work backwards from the desired business outcome: desired outcome → reputation required → actions/proof → what must be learned now. This converts content from an activity into a business-linked system.

### 2. Credibility Bank vs Interest Bank
Use a Credibility Bank when relevant results, projects and solved problems exist. Use an Interest Bank when still experimenting and learning. Do not present unearned expertise as authority.

### 3. Creator Vision
Three dimensions: WHAT (message plus skill, mindset, lifestyle and passions); WHO (demographic and psychographic audience); WHY (story and uniqueness using Problem → Pursuit → Payoff).

### 4. Association and belief matrices
Define associations to reinforce, associations to avoid, common market beliefs and an evidence-based counter-position. Label hypotheses as hypotheses.

### 5. Content scripting
Reusable structures: Hook → Body → CTA; hook alignment (visual + textual + verbal); Callout → Credibility → Compass → Core Learning; Hook → Problem → Journey → Lesson → CTA; and question loops. Optimize for comprehension and retention, not empty engagement.

### 6. Brand scaling
Underlying operating principles across stages: early = narrow proposition, habit, evidence and fast feedback; growth = systems, trust, team leverage and experimentation; scale = specialized production/distribution; mature media = reduce key-person risk through organizational brands/talent. Treat follower thresholds in the source as source-specific heuristics, not universal laws.

### 7. Measurement
Track production inputs, retention/attention, qualified inbound, conversion, audience quality and experiment performance. Do not optimize follower count alone.

### 8. Offer-content alignment
Align content, audience, trust level and monetization. Candidate ladder: free content → lead magnet → low-ticket → mid-ticket → high-ticket. Delivery can be framed as DIY → DWY → DFY where justified.

### 9. Native conversion
Prefer CTAs and offers that are contextually relevant to the problem being taught. Measure qualified outcomes and trust effects rather than assuming native conversion is always superior.
